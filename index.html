Walkputer is a pocket computer fantasy console. Like other fantasy consoles, it's a small computer with simple, fixed limits. It includes its own code editor, compiler and tools, so programs are written and built on the device itself.

It runs on microcontrollers like the Raspberry Pi Pico, in a Game Boy-like form factor: a D-pad and buttons under a 320×240 screen.

Walkputer is coming soon. Updates on the hardware, the system and its apps are posted in the news — you can also follow @walkputer on X or @walkputer on Instagram.
